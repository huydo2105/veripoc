THE SHARED WORKFLOW

A claim becomes reviewable evidence.

AI agents and researchers investigate; VeriPoC independently checks the submitted proof of concept.

  1. 01

    Authorize

    A DeFi team defines what contracts researchers may investigate and under which testing rules. Program enforcement is planned for Issue #35.

  2. 02

    Investigate

    Researchers use their preferred AI agents, security tools, or manual expertise to form a claim and executable PoC.

  3. 03

    Verify

    VeriPoC runs the reviewed PoC in an isolated Foundry worker and returns an evidence-backed verdict for team review.

WHY REPRODUCTION MATTERS

A finding needs more than a confident description.

A vulnerability claim is a hypothesis. A successful Foundry test demonstrates an outcome under specified conditions. A team still decides scope, severity, remediation, and any bounty separately.
